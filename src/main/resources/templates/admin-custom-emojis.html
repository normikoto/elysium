People use an emoji by writing its shortcode between colons, such as :blobcat:, in a post, a display name, a bio, or a profile field. Other servers are sent its image along with what uses it. An image can be PNG, GIF, WebP, or JPEG, of up to 256 KB, and is kept as WebP, animated if it was.

Removing an emoji shows its shortcode as text wherever it was used, here and on servers that fetch those posts again. Other servers keep the copies they were sent.

Import a pack

A pack can be a Misskey export, with a meta.json, a Pleroma or Akkoma pack, with a pack.json, or a .zip or .tar.gz of images, which are named after their files. Misskey's emoji keep their categories, and other packs' are put in a category named after the pack. Each image is checked as an upload is. A pack can be up to 50 MB, with up to 1000 files. Use the import-emojis command for larger ones, or if importing takes long enough for your proxy to give up.
