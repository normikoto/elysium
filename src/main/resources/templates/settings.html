Profile

A field whose value is only a link that starts with https:// is marked as verified if that page links back to your profile with rel="me".

Images can be PNG, JPEG, GIF, or WebP. Avatars are cropped to a square, and headers to three times as wide as they are tall. Backgrounds aren't cropped, and are shown behind your profile here and in apps that support them, but not on other servers.

Privacy
